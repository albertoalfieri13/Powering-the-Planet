# Powering the Planet

## Project Overview

Powering the Planet explores the global energy transition and its relationship with economic development and environmental impact.

Using historical data from Our World in Data, this project investigates how the global energy mix has evolved over time and how economic growth relates to CO₂ emissions.

The analysis focuses on:
- The evolution of the global energy mix, including coal, oil, gas, nuclear and renewable energy.
- The relationship between GDP and CO₂ emissions across countries and regions.
- Differences in CO₂ emissions per capita and their implications for understanding environmental impact.
